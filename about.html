About HEKSAA

Technology supporting mental health monitoring.

HEKSAA is a prototype platform designed to support continuous mental health monitoring, distress reporting, and communication between patients and healthcare professionals.

🎯 Our Purpose

HEKSAA focuses on creating a digital monitoring workflow where patients can report their current mental health state and healthcare professionals can review changes over time.

⚙️ How HEKSAA Works

👤 Patient Check-ins

Patients provide simple self-reported mood, stress, and anxiety information.

📊 Monitoring

Recent responses can be reviewed to identify reported changes over time.

🚨 Distress Alerts

Patients can request attention when they need additional support.

👨‍⚕️ Clinical Interface

Doctors and counselors can review patient information and alerts.

🧠 AI & Risk Prediction

The current dashboard contains prototype indicators for demonstration purposes. Future development can integrate properly validated machine-learning models and clinical evaluation.
