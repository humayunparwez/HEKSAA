Privacy & Disclaimer

Your information and responsible use.

This page explains the current prototype's limitations and how mental health information should be treated.

🔐 Data Privacy

The current HEKSAA frontend prototype stores demonstration information locally in the browser. Production deployment should use secure, authenticated backend storage with appropriate access controls.

📋 Self-Reported Information

Mental health information displayed by HEKSAA is based on information reported by the patient. Self-reported information may not represent a complete clinical assessment.

⚠️ Prototype Indicators

Risk scores, concern levels, trends, and other indicators currently shown in the prototype are demonstrations of the proposed system workflow. They are not medical diagnoses or clinically validated assessments.

🚨 Emergency Disclaimer

HEKSAA is not an emergency response service and should not be relied upon as a substitute for emergency services or professional healthcare. In an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service or qualified professional.

🔒 Future Security

Future versions are intended to include secure authentication, database access controls, appropriate encryption, audit mechanisms, and privacy-aware handling of sensitive information.
