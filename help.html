How can we help?

Find information about using HEKSAA, submitting check-ins, distress alerts, and accessing support.

🧠 Mental Health Check-ins

Patients can regularly report their mood, stress, and anxiety levels from the Patient Dashboard. These responses help track changes over time.

🚨 Distress Support

If you are experiencing significant distress, you can use the "I Need Help" option on the Patient Dashboard to notify the connected doctor or counselor.

👨‍⚕️ Doctor / Counselor Dashboard

Healthcare professionals can review patient check-ins, monitoring trends, risk indicators, and reported distress alerts.

🔐 Account & Privacy

HEKSAA is designed to handle mental health information carefully. Production authentication, secure storage, and access controls will be connected during backend development.

⚠️ Immediate Assistance

HEKSAA is a monitoring prototype and does not replace emergency services or professional care. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.
